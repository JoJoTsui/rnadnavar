# Require explicit evidence and preserve rescue decision provenance

Consensus/rescue outputs are training labels. Missing alternate depth cannot establish that a positive read-support threshold was met, and unconfirmed RNA-dependent evidence cannot erase an established negative DNA classification. We therefore require available valid AD for positive-floor caller votes, distinguish observed/eligible/Somatic support, and gate verification only after biological classification. This may reduce recall where callers omit evidence; retaining unknown measurements and evaluating paired truth transitions is preferable to silently inventing support. Per-caller observations are preserved as versioned, conflict-retaining paired-v2 evidence, while legacy summary fields remain compatibility views. Rescue composition fractions use explicit denominators and are not labeled biological effectiveness. User approved these trade-offs on 2026-09-09; the maintained rules are in [the evidence contract](../CONSENSUS_RESCUE_EVIDENCE_CONTRACT.md).
